Cutroom privacy policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Cutroom edits your videos on your iPhone. We built it so your footage never has to leave your phone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your videos. Cutroom reads the clips you film or pick from Photos, and saves your cuts there too. Videos you pick from Photos stay in Photos; Cutroom refers to them in place rather than copying them, unless you tell it to.
  • Your voice and words. Auto listens to your video with Apple's on-device speech recognition to find pauses, filler words, false starts and retakes. The transcript is stored with your draft on your iPhone.
  • Titles and suggestions. Cutroom names your drafts and reviews cuts with Apple's on-device models, on your iPhone.
  • Your free cuts. Cutroom keeps a count of the free Auto cuts you've used in your iPhone's Keychain, so it isn't reset by deleting and reinstalling the app. The count never leaves your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Cutroom has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't receive your videos, your transcripts or any information about how you use the app.

When your video leaves your iPhone

Only when you send it: to TikTok, to Photos, or through the share sheet to another app. From then on, that app's privacy policy applies.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We don't see your payment details. Apple tells the app which plan you have so it can unlock it.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: to film takes in the Camera tab.
  • Photos: to import clips and save your finished cuts.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe your videos on your iPhone.

You can change any of these in the Settings app.

Children

Cutroom isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/nsalerni/cutroom/issues. A support email is coming soon.
