Cutroom support

Cutroom cuts the pauses, filler words and retakes out of your talking-head videos, right on your iPhone.

Get help

Open an issue at github.com/nsalerni/cutroom/issues and we'll get back to you. A support email is coming soon.

Subscriptions

  • Cutroom includes 3 free Auto cuts to try it. After that, Starter, Pro and Unlimited plans are monthly or yearly, and yearly plans start with a 3-day free trial.
  • To cancel or change a plan, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or in Cutroom, go to Settings and tap Manage subscription.
  • Bought a plan on another iPhone? In Cutroom, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases.
  • For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles all payments.

Common questions

  • Do my videos get uploaded? No. Cutroom edits on your iPhone and only sends a video when you share it.
  • A cut took off part of a word. Tap Cuts in the editor and drag that cut open, or undo it. Every cut stays editable.
  • Auto isn't using AI. Titles and the smarter cut review need Apple Intelligence turned on in the Settings app. Without it, Auto still cuts pauses and retakes.
